CarPlay

Everything your iPhone does, on the big screen.

Maps, music, podcasts, messages, calls and Siri — arranged by CarPlay itself, filling the Tesla's display. It connects the moment you get in, with the phone still in your pocket.

wirelessfull screen60 fps touchnight modeSiri & calls

Connects on its own

Unlock the car and CarPlay is waiting. No cable, no menu to dig through, no pairing dance every time you drive.

Touch that feels like the car's own

Tap, swipe and scroll straight on the Tesla's screen. Dragging a map or scrubbing a playlist tracks your finger the way it should.

Goes dark when the car does

Switch the Tesla to its night look and CarPlay follows in the same moment — no glare on a night drive, no setting to remember.

Sound, calls and Siri through the car

Audio rides the Bluetooth link the car already has with your phone, so music, calls and Siri come out of the car's speakers exactly as they should.

Picks itself back up

If the connection ever drops, it reconnects on its own and carries on. Nothing to reset from the driver's seat.

Bookmark it once

It lives at the same address every time. Save it as a favourite and CarPlay is one tap from the Tesla's home screen.

Games

Parked, it's a games console.

Charging stops are long. Tap Games and the screen fills with the classics — full-size, in colour, with sound through the car's speakers.

on-screen controlsBluetooth gamepadsound in the car starts in seconds

Play on the touchscreen

A D-pad, buttons and shoulders appear over the game — everything you need is on the glass, nothing extra to bring along.

Or bring a real controller

Pair a Bluetooth gamepad and play properly, with the thumbsticks and triggers the games were made for.

Sound through the speakers

Music and effects come through the car's audio, so a parked car turns into a surprisingly good place to play.

There when you want it, gone when you don't

Games stay off until you tap them, and shut down completely when you leave — CarPlay is never waiting behind them.

Every day

One address. No account. No subscription.

It behaves like part of the car: on when the car is on, in the same place every time, and adjustable without ever getting out or reaching for a laptop.

Ready before you've pulled out

It starts with the car and is up in seconds — the screen is ready by the time you've done your seatbelt.

Doesn't need a signal

Everything on the screen comes from the car and your phone. Underground car parks and dead spots make no difference.

Set it up from the driver's seat

Rename its hotspot, choose a password, join a Wi-Fi network — all from the same screen, on the touchscreen, with no keyboard or laptop involved.

See how it's doing

A Monitor screen shows what's connected, how long it's been running and how it's holding up — a glance tells you everything is fine.

Day and night, automatically

The whole interface — not just CarPlay — follows the car between its light and dark looks.

Yours to change

Free, open source, and nothing phones home. Use it as it comes or make it your own.

Getting one

Three things and an afternoon.

Everything runs on a small computer that lives in the glovebox. The setup guide walks the whole thing end to end.

The computer Raspberry Pi 4

Small enough for the glovebox, powered from the car.

The adapter Carlinkit CPC200-CCPA

The wireless CarPlay adapter the Pi talks to.

The phone Your iPhone

iOS 16 or later. Nothing to install on it.

Set up the Pi

Follow the setup guide once, at your desk. It covers every step from a blank memory card to a working box.

02

Put it in the car

Join its Wi-Fi and open the address
