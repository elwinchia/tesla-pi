Cabin mic probe

1 · Conditions

Tap what the car is doing right now — it goes in the report. The interesting run is in Drive: the camera is parked-only, and nobody has confirmed whether the microphone is too.

2 · Microphone

The car should show a permission prompt. Choose Allow.

4 · Can it cancel our own audio?

The car plays a tone out loud for a few seconds, twice over. Set the media volume to a normal listening level first — if the microphone cannot hear the tone at all, the measurement means nothing.

not run

This is the test that decides whether the browser microphone can carry a call at all. With no cancellation the far end hears their own voice coming back out of the cabin speakers.

5 · Does it survive leaving the page?

Then switch to navigation or the media player for ~15 seconds and come back here. If the microphone stops when the page isn't on screen, a phone call can't be held no matter what else works.

6 · Does the permission stick?

After a reload it should say granted without prompting again. Worth repeating after the car has slept.

7 · The cancellation modes this Chromium offers

Section 4 only asks for echoCancellation: true, which cancels the far end of a WebRTC call and nothing else. This car also offers "all" — cancel everything the system renders, which is what our own audio is. Each mode gets the same tone, then a few seconds where you talk, so a mode that cancels our audio can be told apart from one that returns a dead stream. Keep the media volume where you had it for section 4.

not run

A mode is usable for calls only if it hears you and not us.

8 · Send it back

Saves the report (and the recording) on the Pi so nothing has to be read off this screen. Needs MIC_PROBE_DIR set on the server.
