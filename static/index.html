Connect only while this page is open

The CarPlay adapter has its own Wi-Fi, and your phone joins it as soon as it is in range — which takes the phone off your home Wi-Fi and keeps its radios busy even when you are indoors. With this on, the adapter stays closed until you open this page in the car, and closes again about a minute after you leave it. The cost is a short wait for CarPlay each time you get in. Turn it off to have your phone connect the moment you walk up to the car.

Firmware

Updating can take CarPlay away for good. Newer Carlinkit builds encrypt the USB link this app reads, and it only speaks the older plain one — a dongle that lands on such a build stops working here, and there is nothing this end can do about it. Builds marked no rollback cannot be taken back to an earlier one. Every upstream release note reads “Bug Fixes and Improvements”.

    — —

    USB stick

    Plug a FAT32 stick into the Pi, pick a downloaded build above and a stick below, then write. The stick still has to be carried to the dongle by hand: that is the only way Carlinkit let firmware in.

      How to flash

      1. Write the build to a FAT32 stick, above. It lands in the stick's root as A15W_Update.img.
      2. Unplug the stick from the Pi, and unplug the dongle.
      3. Power the dongle from a USB wall charger — not the car, not the Pi — and wait for a steady red light.
      4. Insert the stick. Red and blue alternate for three to four minutes, then settle back to red.
      5. Remove the stick and plug the dongle back into the Pi. This page shows the new version once it reconnects.

          Read telemetry from Bluetooth

          Lets Status › Car show your battery, charge, doors, tyres and temperatures, read over Bluetooth from the car itself. Nothing is sent anywhere — it never leaves this device. Your car allows only three Bluetooth connections at once and phones normally hold them, so this only connects for a few seconds when you tap Read, and never on its own. If a reading fails, turn Bluetooth off on a phone or watch nearby and try again. Turning this off stops the device using Bluetooth for the car entirely.
