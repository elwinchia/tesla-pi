Simulator

Hold Accelerator to drive a pretend car and hear the engine pull through the gears; Brake to slow it. When it has stopped, the engine follows the car's GPS again. Nothing is saved. To rev on the spot, hold the rev meter above.

Car sound

Tesla (the default) is the car as it is: no engine sound, the gauges still following it. Pick any other car to hear it, here and under CarPlay; it starts at your first tap (browsers only let a page make sound after one). Hold the gauges (away from the rev meter) to open these settings.

Each engine plays loops rendered with engine-sim, a physically modelled engine, at idle and nine rev points, each pulling and lifting off, crossfaded and pitched to the engine's revs.

Extras for the recorded mix, each on its own so you can hear what it does. Off is the mix as it was. Each one loads more sound the first time.

A loop every ~450 rpm instead of ~900, so each is stretched less and the exhaust's resonances stop sliding with the revs.

Loops at part throttle between full throttle and lifting off, for cruising and gentle pulls.

The rev limiter cutting the ignition, and the cut of a gear change, rendered by engine-sim.

4 s loops instead of 1.5 s, so the sound never audibly repeats.

engine-sim's physics running in this browser as you drive: true part throttle, transitions and limiter, nothing looped. It needs a lot of processor. If it cannot keep up, the recorded mix plays instead and this says so.

How finely it simulates: lower needs less processor and sounds a little rougher.
