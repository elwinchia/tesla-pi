Gauge style

The rev meter and speedometer, here and under CarPlay. Each is our own drawing in the style of a classic car's cluster, after Blackbox Infinite's "CarPlay Generations" concept. The water and oil temperatures, oil pressure, volts and fuel are made up; speed, trip and odometer are the car's GPS speed.
